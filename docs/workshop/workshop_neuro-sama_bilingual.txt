[h1]Neuropet – Neuro-sama Screen Pet[/h1]
[i]A fan-made HUD mascot for Left 4 Dead 2 · v0.5.2[/i]

Between zombie fights, a tiny Neuro-sama hangs out around the edges of your screen. She walks, trots, or slowly crawls along the bottom; climbs up the sides, travels along the top, hangs there for a while, and sometimes lets go and drops back down. Now and then she trips over, or stops for a hug.

This is an unofficial fan project. It only changes what's drawn on your HUD and doesn't affect gameplay.

[h2]What she does[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Moves around:[/b] walks, trots or crawls along the bottom; climbs the left and right edges; moves along and hangs from the top.
[*][b]Rests in 8 ways:[/b] standing blink, sitting blink, lying down lazily, sitting and looking up, wink, heart hands, shy (standing), shy (sitting).
[*][b]Little moments:[/b] a trip-and-get-back-up animation, and a short hug animation.
[*][b]Not a fixed loop:[/b] route, speed, rest time, pose and little moments are picked at random, and she carries on from wherever she last stopped.
[/list]

[h2]Three packages, pick one[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Neuro-sama[/b] (this item)
[*][b]Evil Neuro[/b]: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3820220066
[*][b]Duo[/b]: Neuro-sama and Evil Neuro each roam on their own and sometimes find each other for a hug. https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3820220607
[/list]
Want both on screen? Choose Duo instead of enabling two single pets.

[h2]Before you subscribe[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Pick one package.[/b] Enable only one Neuropet package (Neuro-sama, Evil Neuro or Duo). Restart after switching; avoid duplicate manual and Workshop copies.
[*][b]Third-party custom HUD mods.[/b] This addon replaces scripts/hudlayout.res, which many custom HUD mods also change, so it doesn't work together with many of them. If you use a custom HUD, you'll need to choose between it and Neuropet.
[*][b]She walks in front of your HUD.[/b] She strolls right along the bottom edge and may pass in front of the health bars. That's on purpose w
[*][b]Made for 16:9 screens.[/b] Other aspect ratios haven't been checked yet.
[/list]

[h2]What it doesn't do[/h2]
She's a little animation, not a game companion: no dragging or clicking, no AI, chat, voice or online features, and she doesn't read or react to in-game events. If she happens to fall right as a Tank shows up, that's just very good timing.

[h2]FAQ[/h2]
[b]Who's the other character in the hug?[/b]
That's her twin sister Evil Neuro, making a guest appearance just for the hug. She's part of the animation, not a second pet.

[b]I haven't seen the trip or the hug yet.[/b]
They're random and can take a few minutes to show up.

[b]Does the server need to install anything?[/b]
No. It runs on your own game through the built-in HUD and material system, with no server scripts and no external programs.

[b]Will she show up on every server?[/b]
That depends on the server. Some servers don't allow custom client addons.

[b]I subscribed, but I can't see her.[/b]
First check whether another HUD mod is enabled. If so, disable it and restart the game. Enable only one Neuropet package (Neuro-sama, Evil Neuro or Duo).

[h2]A few words from us[/h2]
We know, we know: L4D2 is a game where you really should be watching out for Hunters, not a little girl hanging off the top of your screen. We made it anyway, because she's just too cute.

As far as we could find, there wasn't a screen pet like this for L4D2 before, so this might be one of the first. The source code is on GitHub, so if you'd like to make your own pet, you don't have to start from zero. (The code is MIT; the artwork keeps its own license, see below.)

[h1]Neuropet – Neuro-sama 屏幕小宠物[/h1]
[i]Left 4 Dead 2 非官方粉丝 HUD 小伙伴 · v0.5.2[/i]

打僵尸的间隙，屏幕周围会有个小小的 Neuro-sama。她会沿着底边走路、小跑或慢慢趴着爬；会爬上屏幕两侧，沿着顶部移动、挂在上面休息，偶尔手一松掉下来。有时还会自己绊一跤，或者停下来抱抱。

这是非官方的粉丝作品，只改变 HUD 上显示的画面，不影响游戏玩法。

[h2]她会做什么[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]到处走走：[/b]沿底边走路、小跑或慢爬；攀爬左右边缘；沿顶部移动和悬挂。
[*][b]八种休息姿势：[/b]站立眨眼、坐下眨眼、趴下偷懒、坐着抬头、Wink、比心、站着害羞、坐着害羞。
[*][b]小插曲：[/b]绊倒再爬起来，以及一小段抱抱动画。
[*][b]不是固定循环：[/b]路线、速度、休息时间、姿势和小插曲都会随机抽取，下一段从上次停下的地方继续。
[/list]

[h2]三款选一款[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Neuro-sama 单宠版[/b]（本条目）
[*][b]Evil Neuro 单宠版[/b]：https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3820220066
[*][b]Duo 双宠版[/b]：Neuro-sama 和 Evil Neuro 各自活动，偶尔去找对方抱抱。https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3820220607
[/list]
想让两只一起出现，请选择 Duo，无需同时启用两款单宠。

[h2]订阅前请看[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]三款选一款：[/b]每次只启用一款 Neuropet（Neuro-sama、Evil Neuro 或 Duo），切换后重启；工坊订阅与手动安装不要重复。
[*][b]第三方自定义 HUD Mod：[/b]本插件会替换 scripts/hudlayout.res，很多自定义 HUD 插件也会修改这个文件，所以通常无法和它们同时使用。如果你在用自定义 HUD，需要在它和本插件之间选一个。
[*][b]她会从 HUD 前面走过：[/b]她贴着屏幕底边散步，可能会从血条前面经过。不过这是故意的w
[*][b]按 16:9 屏幕制作：[/b]其他屏幕比例暂未确认。
[/list]

[h2]她不会做的事[/h2]
她只是一段小动画，不是游戏助手：不能拖拽或点击，没有 AI、聊天、语音或联网功能，也不会读取或响应游戏事件。如果她刚好在 Tank 出现时掉下来，那只是时机太巧啦。

[h2]常见问题[/h2]
[b]抱抱画面里的另一个角色是谁？[/b]
那是她的双胞胎姐妹 Evil Neuro，专门为抱抱友情客串。她只是动画的一部分，不是第二只宠物。

[b]一直没看到绊倒或抱抱？[/b]
它们是随机出现的，可能要等几分钟。

[b]服务器需要安装什么吗？[/b]
不需要。它只在你自己的游戏里，通过游戏自带的 HUD 和材质系统运行，没有服务器脚本，也没有外部程序。

[b]所有服务器都能看到她吗？[/b]
要看服务器的设置，有些服务器不允许使用自定义的客户端附加内容。

[b]订阅了却看不到她？[/b]
先看看是否启用了其他 HUD 插件。如果有，请停用后重启游戏。也请确认每次只启用一款 Neuropet（Neuro-sama、Evil Neuro 或 Duo）。

[h2]我们的碎碎念[/h2]
我们也知道，在一个本该时刻提防 Hunter 的游戏里，放一个挂在屏幕顶上的小家伙，好像有点奇怪。但她实在太可爱了，所以我们还是做了。

就我们查到的范围，L4D2 之前好像还没有这样的屏幕小宠物，这也许是最早的几个之一。源码放在 GitHub 上，如果你也想做自己的小宠物，不用从零开始。（代码采用 MIT，角色图片沿用各自的许可，见下方。）
