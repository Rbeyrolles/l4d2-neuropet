[h1]Neuropet – Neuro-sama Screen Pet (Fan-made HUD Mascot)[/h1]
Between zombie fights, a tiny Neuro-sama hangs out around your screen edges. She walks, trots or slowly crawls along the bottom, climbs the sides, moves along the top and hangs there for a while. Sometimes she lets go and falls, trips and gets back up, or stops for a hug.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Random routes, speeds, stopping points and eight rest poses.
[*]Walking, trotting, slow crawling, wall climbing, ceiling movement and hanging, falls, trips and hugs.
[*]Evil Neuro makes a guest appearance in the hug; she is part of that animation, not a second pet.
[/list]

[h2]Before subscribing[/h2]
[list]
[*]Enable only one pet edition. Restart after switching; avoid duplicate manual and Workshop copies.
[*]Third-party HUD compatibility is not guaranteed. Addons replacing scripts/hudlayout.res may hide the pet or override the HUD. The stock HUD is recommended; disable other HUD mods and restart if the pet is missing.
[*]Built for 16:9. The pet can overlap HUD elements; no HUD avoidance is included.
[*]Client-only cosmetic addon. No server installation or external program is needed, but servers may restrict client addons.
[*]No AI, chat, voice, mouse interaction or precise game-event reactions. Trips and hugs are random and can take a while to appear.
[/list]

[h1]Neuropet – Neuro-sama 屏幕小宠物[/h1]
打僵尸的间隙，屏幕周围会有个小小的 Neuro-sama。她沿底边走路、小跑或慢慢趴着爬，爬上屏幕两侧，沿顶部移动、挂着休息，偶尔手一松掉下来。有时会绊一跤再爬起来，或者停下来抱抱。

[h2]她会做什么[/h2]
[list]
[*]路线、速度、停留位置和八种休息姿势随机变化。
[*]走路、小跑、慢爬、攀墙、沿顶部移动和悬挂、跌落、绊倒与抱抱。
[*]抱抱中会有 Evil Neuro 友情客串；她只是动画的一部分，不是第二只宠物。
[/list]

[h2]订阅前请看[/h2]
[list]
[*]每次只启用一版宠物，切换后重启；工坊订阅与手动安装不要重复。
[*]不保证与第三方 HUD Mod 兼容；修改同一份 scripts/hudlayout.res 时，可能使宠物不显示或覆盖界面布局。建议使用原版 HUD；遇到问题先停用其他 HUD Mod 并重启。
[*]默认按 16:9 制作；宠物可能遮挡 HUD，没有自动避让。
[*]客户端观赏版，无需服务器安装或外部程序；部分服务器可能限制客户端插件。
[*]没有 AI、聊天、语音、鼠标互动或精确的游戏事件联动。绊倒与抱抱随机出现，可能需要等一会儿。
[/list]
