# 封面初稿 v1：生成提示词

制作方式：内置 imagegen。人物参考原抱抱素材，有 AI 辅助放大重绘；背景和标题为封面设计，非实机截图。

## Neuro-sama

```text
Use case: compositing / ads-marketing.
Asset: ONE square 1024 x 1024 Steam Workshop cover draft for an unofficial Left 4 Dead 2 fan HUD pet. This is the Neuro-sama edition of a matching two-cover series.
Input image: the provided transparent Neuron hug10.png is the CENTRAL ART INSERT, not merely a mood reference. Preserve the original two chibi characters and this exact hugging pose, silhouette, outfits, colors, facial expressions, red linework, bows and proportions as closely as possible. The pink/yellow Neuro-sama is actively hugging the dark/red Evil Neuro. Use just this pair once. Keep the full bodies and feet visible. Retain the charming simple flat sprite look, not detailed anime repainting, 3D or realism. Do not introduce new characters, limbs, props or costumes.
Design: a gentle, cozy, polished collectible sticker/postcard cover. Clean warm cream background with subtle soft blush shading and a restrained mint accent, airy and uncluttered. A very faint soft blush oval behind the pair can separate them from the background. No game environment, zombies, weapons, actual HUD screenshot, horror imagery or fake Steam interface. No bright white sticker outline around the characters.
Layout: safe margins approximately 7 percent. Large neat friendly rounded bold lettering across the top, exactly "NEUROPET". Central hug artwork takes most of the remaining space. Clearly legible centered character label below it, exactly "NEURO-SAMA". A small but readable bottom line, exactly "L4D2 · HUD PET". These are the only words; do not add a tagline, official badge, artist signature or watermark. Strong text contrast with the cream ground; use muted dark warm ink for the big letters, restrained muted mint for a small accent. The main character label must remain readable as a small square thumbnail. Balanced, affectionate, not overly glossy or busy.
Output a finished flat square cover image, not a photographed card, device mockup, montage, comparison sheet or several covers.
```
